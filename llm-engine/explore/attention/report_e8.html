E8 — Per-head attention temperature, on a real model

llm-engine/explore/attention · e8_attention_temperature.py · frozen diagfix, real 40-token window

What was done

Would a learnable softmax temperature per head (idea from the E2 report) actually have a lever to pull? Using the identity

softmax(τ·scores) == softmax(τ·log(w)) for recorded weights w

we re-weighted every recorded head on a real window over five temperatures (0.25, 0.5, 1.0, 2.0, 4.0) and computed, per (layer, head): entropy and peak mass at each τ, and the smallest τ at which a head turns "sharp" (peak ≥ 0.30). No engine change; this is an inference-time simulation.

Reading: every single head has a temperature lever. At the model's default τ=1 exactly zero heads are "sharp". But a modest τ=4 turns all eight heads sharp on the same weights — the "sharp heads don't exist" conclusion of E2 is a consequence of one shared τ was constrained by training, not of the model lacking the internal representation. A learned per-head temperature has huge, immediate headroom.

Interesting / worrying

  • Layer-0 heads need τ=2 to cross sharp; layer-1 heads need τ=4. The two layers have different "pre-trained scale", so a single global τ could never serve both recruiting and summarising — strong argument for per-head, or at least per-layer, temperatures.
  • At τ=0.25 all heads become near-uniform (entropy ~0.975 for all 8): the model carries no head that is forced to read just one token; even the "diffuse" mode can be reshaped by temperature.
  • This is a probe performed on the frozen model; it doesn't guarantee a trained τ would land on these values, but it bounds the achievable range: the sharp signal is present and reachable, which is exactly what E2's one-τ view could not show.

Why it would be a good addition

  • Per-head temperature parameter. One float/head multiplied into the logits before softmax (in the fused kernel path, pre-softmax ÷τ on the raw scores). ~5 lines in the attention forward; default 1.0 keeps behaviour identical until warmup.
  • A self-calibrating range. Target range from this table — default τ=1.0, allow 0.25…4 — so heads can specialize early, avoiding the "diffuse-only" end-state from E2.
  • Diagnostics missing-piece. The entropy-vs-τ curve is a great recovery signal: a shrinking max-reachable-peak as training proceeds tells you the head has no residual sharpness left.

Verdict: a 5-line engine change with a fibre-optic view — this is the experiment to propose next on the GPU box.

How it changes the workflow

  • Head specialisation becomes something you can actually shape: a single per-head "temperature" knob turns the E2 head-visualisation into a source of actionable information.
  • Eval: report per-head τ alongside today's entropy column to separate "heads that are dead" from "heads that are merely temperature-suppressed" — the former get pruned, the latter get a temperature reset rather than a rewrite.
