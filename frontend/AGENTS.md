# Slate presentation

Read the current [design contract](../docs/frontend/design.md) and
[appearance tokens](../docs/frontend/appearance.md) before changing presentation.

Default to no explanatory subtext. Do not add generic subtitles, background
mechanics, reassurance or copy that repeats a heading. Examples to reject:
“Drafts save in this browser”, “Manage all your models in one place” and
“Configure the settings below”. Keep authored descriptions, consequential field
hints, units, recovery actions and real states such as Unsaved changes, Paused,
Legacy or 2 waiting.

Use the shared list, header, field, menu and notification contracts. Do not add
decorative header/list/stat separators, standalone status dots or blurred shadow
halos. Benchmark presentation remains outside the current redesign.
