## Description

FineWeb-Edu is CommonCrawl filtered by an educational-quality classifier. That
filter is why it was chosen — it is the cheapest source of varied *real* English
at volume — but it is also a single model's opinion about what good text looks
like, applied to every token. At 1.5 GB out of 1.5 GB it was the only option; at
~50 GB it should not still be the only general-web view.

**Wanted.** `scripts/prepare_webtext.py` on the TODO-0111 helper, writing
sharded text to `data/input/WebText` under a `--mb` budget. Target ~10 GB.

**Source.** `allenai/c4` and `mlfoundations/dclm-baseline-1.0` both exist and
were verified during planning. They differ in the way that matters here:

- **C4** is heuristically cleaned (deduplication, bad-word and boilerplate
  filters) with no learned quality model — maximally *different* from
  FineWeb-Edu's classifier, which is the whole point of this child.
- **DCLM-baseline** is itself model-filtered and is the stronger corpus by
  benchmark, but its filtering is closer in spirit to FineWeb-Edu's, so it
  buys less diversity per gigabyte.

C4 is the better fit for the stated goal; DCLM is the better fit if raw quality
wins. Pick one, and state which and why in the docstring — this is the one
decision in this child that is not mechanical. Note that C4's `en` config is
sharded into many small files and DCLM is very large, so the lazy per-shard
fetch from the helper matters more here than anywhere else.

**Overlap with FineWeb is real and mostly fine.** Both derive from CommonCrawl,
so some documents will appear in both datasets and the pretrain corpus will see
them twice. Cross-dataset deduplication at this scale is its own project and is
out of scope. Say so in the docstring rather than leaving the next reader to
wonder whether it was considered.

## Definition of done

- The chosen repo, its verified schema, and the reason it was chosen over the

### Builder · 2026-08-10
Added `scripts/prepare_webtext.py` on `allenai/c4` (`en`). C4 over DCLM-baseline with the reasoning in the docstring: C4 is heuristically cleaned with no learned quality model, so it is maximally *different* from FineWeb-Edu's classifier, and diversity is this dataset's entire job. DCLM is the stronger corpus but its filtering rhymes with FineWeb's, and it ships `.jsonl.zst` needing a dependency this repo lacks.

C4 is gzipped JSON lines, not parquet, which is why `corpus_shards.iter_jsonl_rows` exists. The known CommonCrawl overlap with FineWeb is documented as accepted rather than left unexplained. Verified with a real 3 MB fetch (835 documents).
