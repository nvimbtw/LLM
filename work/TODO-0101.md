## Description

`autolabel.rs` (626 lines) and `gather.rs` (497) are two strategies for the same
job: produce candidate replies that become preference data in
`raw/preference/`. `autolabel` generates several candidates per prompt and lets
`llm::autolabel::Judge` pick a best and a worst by rule; `gather` generates one
reply per prompt and hands it to a person. `gather.rs`'s own header documents
the pairing explicitly, and the two exist to produce different data on purpose.

They belong in one crate — `labeling` — rather than two, because neither is
meaningful without the concept the other half defines, and splitting them would
make the KTO/RAFT thresholds (`MIN_SIGNALS`, `MIN_PER_CLASS`, `MIN_CATALOGUE`,
`MIN_RAFT_EXAMPLES`, all `pub` in `gather.rs`) live in a crate that does not use
them.

`autolabel` depends on `storage` and `llm`; `gather` has no `use crate::` at all
and depends on `llm` and `tokenize`. Neither touches `sqlx` or `actix`.

Move both to `crates/labeling` as two modules (`labeling::autolabel`,
`labeling::gather`). Consumers to rewrite: `queue.rs`, `cycle.rs`,
`routes/jobs.rs`, `routes/signals.rs`, `routes/cycles.rs`.

Confirm before wiring: `gather.rs` exposes `GatherRequest` and the threshold
helpers. Check whether any of its function signatures take a `PgPool` — if one
does, that edge has to go through the caller in `jobs`/`web` instead, because
`labeling` must not depend on `db` (it sits below it in the order).
