## Description

The thinking-mode SFT stage (TODO-0119) trains the model to produce a
`<|thinking|>` block before answering. A base model whose pretrain corpus
contains no worked reasoning has nothing to put in that block — it will learn
the *shape* of a reasoning trace without the substance. Math text is the
cheapest large source of explicit step-by-step derivation.

**Wanted.** `scripts/prepare_math.py` on the TODO-0111 helper, writing sharded
text to `data/input/Math` under a `--mb` budget.

**Source.** `HuggingFaceTB/finemath` and `open-web-math/open-web-math` both
exist and were verified during planning. finemath is the newer and more heavily
filtered of the two and has quality-scored subsets, which fits the
`--min-score` idiom `prepare_fineweb.py` established. Verify the schema before
writing against it either way.

**Sized smaller than its siblings, on purpose.** This is P3 and the budget
should default well under 10 GB. A model at this scale gets less out of math
than out of general prose, and math text is the most specialized thing in the
corpus — it earns its place by giving the thinking block something to imitate,
not by volume. Start around 2-4 GB and let the flag raise it if a run shows it
paying.

**LaTeX.** Math text is dense in `$…$` and `\begin{align}`. Leave it as-is —
it is legitimate structured content and the model may as well see it — but
check what fraction of documents are *mostly* markup rather than prose and
filter those, the same way `prepare_code.py` filters minified files. A document
that is 90% backslashes teaches the tokenizer more than it teaches the model.

## Definition of done

- `data/input/Math` holds sharded text within the requested `--mb`.
- The default budget is stated and deliberately below the other sources'.
- Markup-dominated documents are filtered by a stated rule.
- The chosen repo and its verified schema are documented.
