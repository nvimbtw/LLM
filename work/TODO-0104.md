## Description

Closing step of EPIC-0094: make the entrypoint and the documentation tell the
truth about the tree.

### main.rs

`main.rs` is already short (85 lines) and already does the right job —
`HttpServer` setup, CORS, `app_data` registration — but it carries fourteen
`mod` declarations for modules that no longer exist by the time this artifact
runs. Remove them, leaving `use` statements pointing at the crates, and confirm
what remains matches the "thin entrypoint only" rule `backend/CLAUDE.md` states
for this file.

After this, `backend/src/` contains `main.rs` and nothing else.

### backend/CLAUDE.md

The "Directory structure" section is currently fiction and has been for some
time. It claims:

- `crates/*` are "Cargo workspace members declared in `backend/Cargo.toml`" —
  there was no workspace and no `crates/` directory until EPIC-0094.
- The members are `crawler`, `data_pipeline`, `tokenization`, `llm` — three of
  those four names do not exist on disk. The real ones were `llm` and
  `tokenize`, both nested under `src/`.
- `migrations/` sits at `backend/migrations/` — it was at
  `backend/src/db/migrations/`, and TODO-0098 moves it to
  `crates/db/migrations/`.

Rewrite the section to match the ten-crate tree, and state the dependency order
explicitly (`storage`/`crypto`/`tokenize` → `llm` → `training`/`synth`/
`labeling` → `db` → `jobs` → `web` → bin) so the next person adding a crate
knows where it goes and what it may depend on. The existing rule "never create a
new crate outside `backend/crates/`" finally becomes accurate — keep it.

The layering rules for `routes`/`db`/`crypto` stay as written; they describe the
contents of those crates correctly and only the paths change.

### Follow-ups to record, not to do

Add a short "known follow-ups" note to the epic's comments rather than acting:
`training.rs` and `synth.rs` still want splitting into modules;
`training::TrainConfig` stutters (R21); ISSUE-0065 and ISSUE-0068 need their
`location` rebased onto `crates/db/`.

## Definition of done

- `ls backend/src/` shows exactly `main.rs`.
- `main.rs` declares no `mod` for anything that is now a crate.
- The "Directory structure" section of `backend/CLAUDE.md` matches the tree,
  names all ten crates, and states the dependency order.
- `cargo build` and `cargo clippy --all-targets` clean at the workspace root;
  `cargo test` passes for every member.
- The app starts, migrations apply against a clean database, and a chat turn
  round-trips.
